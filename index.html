Train and show times here are planning windows. Nothing new has been booked. Approximate pins indicate neighbourhoods, not confirmed entrances; use the Chinese address for last-mile navigation.

  • Hotels: Dec 24–27 Huaguoshan; Dec 27–29 Holiday Inn Express Jiuzhaigou; Dec 29–Jan 1 Moon Crescent. Check the Moon Crescent checkout time: the booking summary’s “12:00 AM” is ambiguous.
  • Rail: 成都东 → 黄龙九寨 on Dec 27; reverse Dec 29. Expected sale-window opening dates are Dec 13 and Dec 15 respectively, subject to station release hours on 12306. Bring the passport used for booking.
  • Road connection: Huanglongjiuzhai station is not at the park. Reserve the onward shuttle/car and allow 2–2½ hours plus waiting and winter delays. Do not book a tight return train connection.
  • Winter park day: Check dated opening areas, tickets and bus operation. Wear warm layers and shoes with grip. Follow closure notices and keep the route flexible.
  • Transport estimates: All city travel ranges are planning allowances, not live routing. Check Amap before leaving; station exits, traffic and winter conditions can change them.
  • Optional places: Qiaoyiqiao and Donglin require a substantial detour. Use them as replacements for a half-day. Xuhucha, MONDOLI, Daqi and Baijiaban are alternatives, not compulsory extra stops.
